CUAHSI piloted its first Virtual Open House on October 22, 2025, creating a dedicated forum for broad community engagement on CUAHSI's direction, programs, and services. The three-hour event drew more than 90 registrants and over 50 unique attendees, participating in a flexible format that allowed attendees to drop in and move between breakout sessions as their schedules allowed.

The event opened with a welcome and organizational update from CEO Jordan Read, followed by a general breakout and five concurrent discussion rooms covering: CUAHSI's 2026–2028 plans, evolving data management and publication, training needs and in-person collaboration opportunities, science collaboration with CUAHSI, and a Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute overview.

Several themes emerged across sessions that are directly informing CUAHSI's planning:

- **HydroShare discovery documentation** — much of CUAHSI's existing user documentation is geared toward publishing data; attendees requested clearer guidance on *finding* and *working with* data that already exists in HydroShare
- **Introductory data management resources** — particularly for students who are new to research and facing data management requirements for the first time
- **Expanded coding and data skills training** — hydrologists want more training at the intersection of domain science and modern data analysis tools
- **Community connection** — attendees emphasized CUAHSI's role in fostering scientific connection and collaboration, not just providing tools

The Virtual Open House format will continue under the new cooperative agreement (EAR-2535162).
