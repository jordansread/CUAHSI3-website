CUAHSI piloted its first Virtual Open House on October 22, 2025, a three-hour event with more than 90 registrants and over 50 unique attendees. CEO Jordan Read opened with organizational updates, followed by five concurrent breakout sessions covering CUAHSI's 2026–2028 plans, HydroShare data management, training needs, science collaboration, and the Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute.

Key themes that emerged and are shaping future planning: clearer documentation for finding and working with data in HydroShare (most existing docs focus on publishing, not discovery); introductory data management resources for students; expanded coding and data skills training; and CUAHSI's role in fostering scientific connection beyond tools.

The Virtual Open House format will continue under EAR-2535162. Watch for the next one in the events calendar.
