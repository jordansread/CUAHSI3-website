As part of CUAHSI's 25th anniversary, this issue opens a series of reflections from people who have helped shape CUAHSI's story over the years. The first invited contributor is **Rick Hooper**, who served as CUAHSI's Executive Director from 2002 to 2017.

Hooper revisits the question NSF posed in 2001 — how a major investment could transform hydrologic science — and the community organizing that led to CUAHSI's creation. He argues it's an opportune time to revisit that question, given the uncertainty currently surrounding NSF funding, and shares lessons learned from CUAHSI's early years.

Additional reflections from community members will continue through the remainder of the year.
