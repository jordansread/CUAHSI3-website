slug: sierra
summary: "A platform and program for investigating how the water cycle drives Critical Zone processes, with a focus on water balance and nutrient cycling."
published: true
sources:
  - https://czo-archive.criticalzone.org/sierra/
---

Its sites include Providence Creek (four instrumented headwater catchments at the rain-snow transition, 1700 to 2000 metres, in mixed-conifer forest) and Wolverton Basin in Sequoia National Park. The archive's note to colleagues says that after the CZO program ended in November 2020, measurements at the main Providence site continue under the USFS Pacific Southwest Research Station, and those at the San Joaquin Experimental Range and Soaproot Saddle continue under NEON.
