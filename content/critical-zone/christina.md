The Christina River Basin, in Delaware and Pennsylvania, includes the Brandywine, White Clay and Red Clay Creeks, has a long agricultural and early industrial history, and is described by the observatory as among the best-studied watersheds in the nation. About half a million people rely on it for drinking water, agriculture and recreation.
