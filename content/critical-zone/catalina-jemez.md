title: "Catalina-Jemez Critical Zone Observatory"
slug: catalina-jemez
summary: "Measures the geomorphic, hydrologic and biogeochemical interactions that drive theory and models of how the Critical Zone evolves, in the arid and semi-arid landscapes of the U.S. Southwest."
published: true
sources:
  - https://czo-archive.criticalzone.org/catalina-jemez/
---

Arrays of sensors and samplers measure the fluxes and fate of water, carbon and lithogenic elements. A stated aim was to set up natural laboratories for the wider earth-science community and to help students and the public understand how Critical Zone processes connect.
