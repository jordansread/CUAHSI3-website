Three basins: Clear Creek in Iowa (intensive agriculture, artificial drainage, wetland loss and urbanization); the Sangamon River Basin in Illinois (farmed since the 1800s, when tallgrass prairie was converted, with changed drainage altering hydrology and biogeochemistry); and the Minnesota River Basin (among the most rapidly incising rivers in the world).
