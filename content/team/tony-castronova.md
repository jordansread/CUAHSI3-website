## Recent work at CUAHSI

- Co-organized the **National Model Evaluation Hackathon** (Princeton University, March 2026) to co-design an open, community-driven framework for evaluating hydrologic model outputs across ParFlow-CONUS, NWM, SUMMA, and other models using shared Jupyter workflows.

- Leading CUAHSI's contribution to the **CIROH HydroShare modernization** initiative, including the transition to cloud-native storage, the DataCite DOI migration, and integration with Repo2Docker and BinderHub for reproducible workflow publishing.

- Co-hosted a workshop with the AGU Publishing team at AGU 2025: *Technical Skills for Publishing and Sharing Data and Code* (with Kimberlee Wong).

- Co-author on **Tarboton et al. (2026)**: Enabling Community Research with the Next Generation National Water Modeling Framework on HydroShare-Linked Computing Platforms. [HydroShare resource](https://www.hydroshare.org/resource/0580878f98d548b08923b780e667c396/)
