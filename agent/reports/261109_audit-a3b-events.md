# A3b: Item-level parity of the events, legacy site against this site

Site audit task A3b (org issue 103, epic 99), 9 November 2026. Read-only on both sites. Legacy side: the 6 November snapshot (`raw/legacy-site/261106/pages/events`, gitignored; 120 pages). New side: `content/events/` (45 files, all published) on the fork's main (b542300), with `content/cyberseminars/` (88 files) as a second target and the legacy `cyberseminars` and `workshops` pages (112 pages) as a third. Outputs in `agent/audit/`: `a3b-legacy-261106.csv` (120 rows), `a3b-new-261106.csv` (45), `a3b-match-261106.csv` (120), `a3b-recurring-261106.csv` (15), `a3b-new-links-261106.csv` (17), `a3b-new-elsewhere-261106.csv` (33), `a3b-hand-match-261106.csv` (20, the control, written before the script was run), `a3b-numbers-261106.json`. Script: `scripts/parity/a3b-events.mjs` (reads the snapshot and the content folders; no network).

## The answer in one paragraph

**The new site has the current events and almost none of the past ones, and not one legacy event address survives the cut-over.** Of the 120 legacy event pages, **14 (12%) have a counterpart on the new site** (10 exact, 4 probable); 12 of those are entries in `content/events/` and the other 2 are cyberseminar items that are now unpublished (finding 4). **106 have none**: 23 are the same title on another date (a recurring event, "weak" below), 1 is "possible" (to be confirmed by a person), 82 have nothing. The split is by year: of the 18 legacy pages dated 2026, 12 match; of the 100 dated 2022 to 2025, **none matches an event** and 2 match an unpublished cyberseminar. The new `/community/events` page has a past-events list, but `content/events/` holds no event before January 2026. One legacy page is an upcoming call that the new site lacks (finding 2). All 120 legacy event addresses would answer 404 after cut-over (A2's simulation), and 15 of the 17 links in new events that point at www.cuahsi.org would too (finding 5).

## Findings

1. **The past is missing, and that is a choice that has not been made.** 100 legacy pages are dated 2022 to 2025 (55 single-date, 27 ranges, 18 deadline-only). The new `/community/events` page lists past events (`pages/community/events/index.vue`), so a past-events archive fits it, but no file in `content/events/` starts before 5 January 2026. Whether the new site should carry 2022 to 2025 events is Jordan's call. 18 of the 100 are deadline pages (an `events` item of type `deadline` exists, and 8 of the new 45 use it; 2 of the 14 matched pairs are deadline pages), so whether past deadlines are worth an entry is part of the same decision.
2. **One upcoming legacy call is missing from the new site:** `/events/call-for-2027-water-prediction-innovators-summer-institute-theme-leaders` ("Application Deadline 12.1.2026"; the page says review begins 1 December 2026 and applications are accepted to 16 December). Of the 4 legacy pages whose date is on or after the crawl day (6 November), 3 are matched (the 2026 AGU meeting, the 2026 membership meeting, the Data Publishing Program, which ends 9 November) and this one is not.
3. **Differences on the matched pairs** (12 pairs to `content/events/`, compared on start, end, time, place, mode and registration link; 4 of the 12 differ, 8 do not on anything compared). Both locations are given and which side is right is Jordan's (P4):
   - `2026-near-surface-geophysics-community-input-workshop`: the legacy page says "August 25, 2026 / 12:00 PM - 12:00 AM MDT" with no end date; the new item runs 25 August 08:30 to 26 August 12:00 MDT. The legacy times look like a placeholder, so the new item is probably right, but I have not seen the organiser's page.
   - `cyberwater2-training` and `data-publishing-program`: the legacy pages have an Apply link (a Jotform address); the new items have no registration link. Both have a deadline on the legacy page (28 August 2026 and 2 October 2026) which is past.
   - `cuahsi-virtual-open-house-2`: the legacy page registers through a Zoom address; the new item's registration address is `https://cuahsi.org/register/open-house`, which redirects to www and **answers 404** (requested twice on 9 November 2026; the output is in `agent/audit/a3b-checks-261109.txt`). The event was on 24 June 2026.
4. **Two matches are to items that are not live.** The legacy May 2024 HydroShare webinar and July 2024 Compute Services webinar match `2024-intro-hydroshare-may` and `2024-intro-compute-services-july` (probable), which PR 209 set to `published: false` because their videos are not public. So 10 legacy pages (7 for HydroShare, 3 for Compute Services) have no live counterpart, and a possible match remains for the 11 March 2025 Cloud Computing webinar (same date as `2025-intro-compute-services-march`; the titles differ: "possible", to be confirmed).
5. **Links in new events point at the legacy site and would break.** 17 values in 15 files of `content/events/` (`location.url` or `registration.url`) are addresses on www.cuahsi.org: 2 are the home page, **15 would answer 404 after cut-over** (12 distinct paths in all; A2's list, `a3b-new-links-261106.csv`), among them the legacy `/events/...` pages of the AGU meeting (cited by 3 files), CyberWater2 and the Data Publishing Program, and `/cyberseminars/...`, `/community/...` and `/workshops/...` pages. They are for A4 (links) to repair; they are listed here because the events are the content.
6. **All 120 legacy event addresses would 404 after cut-over** (A2's class `404` for 120 of 120; the new events live at `/community/events/<slug>`, not `/events/<slug>`, and only 1 of the 14 matched pairs has the same slug on both sites). The 120 pages carry **748 clicks** in the 12-month Search Console export (748 of the 15,918 clicks on www rows in the export, `clicks_total` in `agent/audit/cutover-261106-numbers.json`; the export is cut at its 1,000-row limit, so a page below the cut has no clicks here): 64 on the 14 matched pages, 684 on the 106 that are not. The largest are a 2026 Summer Institute application deadline page (133, weak), a freshwater science meeting of 2025 (127, none) and the 2025 AGU meeting (71, weak).
7. **The legacy date line is wrong on 7 pages** (the legacy site's own display, reported as found): "December 20 - 20, 2025" for the 2025 AGU meeting (the heading says 15 to 19 December) and "December 16 - 16, 2022"; five ranges where the day after the dash is smaller than the first ("October 19 - 9, 2026" for the Data Publishing Program, "October 30 - 1, 2025", "July 22 - 2, 2024", "July 28 - 8, 2025" and "October 25 - 4, 2022"): the second number is a day of the next month, and I read it that way. A migration that copies those dates would copy the mistakes.
8. **Contact details.** 35 of the 120 legacy pages mention a contact or an obscured address ("email hidden"). 2 of the 45 new event files contain an `@` somewhere in the front matter or body. I did not compare who the contact is (P8: no address is in any committed file).

## Controls

- **20 events matched by hand first** (`a3b-hand-match-261106.csv`, written from the page and file lists, before the script existed; nothing committed proves the order, and the notes show I had read the legacy date lines, so this is a check on the script's logic, not an independent one): 10 exact, 2 probable, 1 weak, 7 none. Five hand-file rows had a truncated or wrong page name or file name and were corrected (four before the first run, after I listed the legacy pages; one, the HydroShare workshop, after it found nothing under my name); no level was changed.
- **Agreement:** on the first run 12 of 20 agreed. Three of the differences were defects found by reading the output: the script dropped the word "deadline" from titles, which made "CUAHSI Community Award Nomination" an exact match for "...Nomination Deadline" (the definition says titles must be the same words; fixed); the date reader took a programme's dates from the body of an application-deadline page instead of the date printed under the title (fixed); and the hand slug of one workshop was wrong (above). After the fixes, **15 of 20 agree on the level** and **20 of 20 agree on matched (exact or probable) against not matched**. The sample is weighted to 2026 (12 of the 20 are matched, 10 of them exact) and has only 2 "none" pairs that sit away from a same-title page, so it tests the exact and probable end well and the none/weak boundary (where 82 and 23 of the 120 fall) little. The 5 that differ are my hand verdicts misapplying the scale rather than script errors: all are pairs where I wrote "none" because the other date is not on the site and the scale says "weak" for the same title on another date (the Virtual Open House of October 2025, the 2022 ESIP meeting, the 2025 CIROH conference, the 2026 Summer Institute application deadline, the June 2025 HydroShare webinar). I did not change the hand file or the scale to make them agree.
- **Six read by hand** (legacy page and new file opened): matched, `cyberwater2-training` (legacy dates 21 to 25 September 2026 and an Apply link; the new file has the same dates and no registration key), `2026-near-surface-geophysics-community-input-workshop` (times above; the new file's location and registration addresses are the same RingCentral page that the legacy page links), `cuahsi-virtual-open-house-2` (Zoom against the cuahsi.org address, above); not matched, `call-for-2027-water-prediction-innovators-summer-institute-theme-leaders` (nothing in `content/events/` on or near 1 December 2026), `gsa-connects-2023` (a 15 to 18 October 2023 meeting with one external link; no GSA item on the new site), `introduction-to-hydroshare-webinar-3` (10 June 2025 webinar; the only HydroShare items on the new site are of May 2024, unpublished, and none is dated June 2025).
- **Stopwords:** the title reader ignores the words the, and, for, with, free and part (stated in the definitions), so "Free Webinar: X" and "Webinar: X" on the same date would be exact.
- **New-side files:** all 88 cyberseminar files have both front-matter delimiters (0 of 88 with fewer than 2; `agent/audit/a3b-checks-261109.txt`), so the "9 files with no closing ---" in CLAUDE.md C5 is out of date for this tree; the 45 event files were read the same way.
- **Extraction:** the dates of the 120 pages were parsed from the text under the title; all 118 dated pages parsed, the 2 undated ones are listed; the 7 odd date lines were read in the CSV. Two of those carry a caveat in the match CSV, which uses the printed date: the 2025 AGU meeting is read as 20 December 2025 (its heading says 15 to 19 December, so its `days_apart` is not meaningful) and the "AGU Fall Meeting" page, which prints "December 15" with no year, has no date and could be any year (so it is not in the 4 upcoming).

- **Time zones:** times were compared as printed (legacy "1:00 PM ET" against the clock time in the new file's offset); no conversion was made. 5 of the 12 pairs have a time on both sides; 1 differs (the near-surface one above), and the other 7 have a time on one side only or on neither.
- **The other 28 new events against the legacy listing page `/events`** (a calendar view): not read. The match is made from the 120 individual pages.
- **Whether the 23 new events with no legacy counterpart are right:** they came from newsletters and staff, not from the legacy site; their facts were not checked here (that is the newsletter audit, A3c).
- **Workshops, cyberseminars and the job board** as sections: the cyberseminars were done in A3g; the workshop pages (24) are in A3f. This task read them only to look for a counterpart of a new event.
