Site audit task A3g (org issue 108, epic 99), 6 November 2026. Read-only on both sites. Legacy side: the 6 November snapshot (`raw/legacy-site/261106/`, gitignored). New side: `content/cyberseminars/` on the fork's main (acbff52). Outputs in `agent/audit/`: `a3g-legacy-pages-261106.csv` (88 rows), `a3g-legacy-videos-261106.csv` (one row per video link), `a3g-new-261106.csv` (25 rows), `a3g-match-261106.csv`, `a3g-youtube-261106.csv` (95 video ids checked), `a3g-numbers-261106.json`. ## The answer in one paragraph

**The legacy site holds no recording archive of its own, and the bulk of what it links is a series this site does not have.** The legacy site's 88 cyberseminar pages are 61 event pages (webinars, mostly with a Register link), 6 yearly archive pages of the Women Advancing River Research (WARR) series, 20 series pages and the listing. Only 18 of the 88 pages link any video: the 6 WARR pages (63 videos) and 12 event pages (1 each), 75 distinct YouTube ids in all, **every one of which is public on the CUAHSI channel (75 of 75)**. The new site has 25 cyberseminar files (21 published) and 23 video ids; only **3 ids are on both sites**. So 72 of the legacy site's 75 linked recordings are not on the new site: 63 are WARR, 9 are other webinars. The new site has no WARR series at all, although a 2026 impact story on it says CUAHSI took over that archive. The legacy homepage's "over 150 recorded webinar presentations" cannot be checked from the legacy site (it links 75) and needs the channel's own list (see "What I need").

## Findings

1. **63 of the 75 videos the legacy site links belong to WARR, a series absent from this site.** The six yearly pages are the only archive the legacy site itself holds. Eleven videos were published together on 19 October 2026 (item 7); the legacy WARR page says "The WARR archive is now hosted on CUAHSI's YouTube channel and website." The new site's story `warr-archive-cuahsi-2026` says the same and links the legacy series page.
2. **9 other linked recordings are not on the new site** (a data-sharing session on critical zone science, hydrogeodesy, an Indigenous voices session, technical tools and methods, a federal job market session, three synthesis workshop sessions, and a post-field-season session).
3. **45 of the 61 legacy event pages have no counterpart here (44 "none" and the set-aside "weak"; 1 more is "possible"), and most link no video.** Whether recordings of those 45 exist on the channel is not known: the legacy site does not link them. This needs the channel list.
4. **Two published new items point to videos that are not public.** `intro-compute-services-july-2024` and `intro-hydroshare-may-2024` have ids for which oEmbed answered 403 (not 200), and whose thumbnails answer 404 (the thumbnail of a video that did answer 200, used as a control, answers 200; `a3g-youtube-261106.csv`). The card on `/learn-train/cyberseminars` builds its thumbnail from the id, so a broken picture is likely; whether the player fails when clicked was not seen (embedding disabled is another cause of a 403). What a 403 means is not confirmed: private, deleted or embedding off are all possible, and a person with channel access can tell.
5. **Two unpublished new items carry malformed ids.** `nav-beyond-academic-ai-2025` has `GMeAjRTsM` (9 characters) and `nav-beyond-academic-broader-impacts-2025` has `Y483oWkQ` (8); YouTube ids have 11. Two more unpublished items have no id. The schema accepts any string (`youtube_id: z.string()` in `scripts/content-schemas.mjs`), so nothing would have stopped them going live; a check of the id's shape is a small code change for its own PR (schema changes are code, C1).
6. **The legacy site counts differently from itself and from October.** October's "67 cyberseminar pages and 20 series" were 61 event pages and the 6 WARR yearly pages (and a listing page), not 67 recordings. The legacy cyberseminars page says "All cyberseminars are recorded and available on our YouTube channel" and the homepage says "over 150 recorded webinar presentations"; the site links 75.
7. **The channel is `@CUAHSI`** (channel id `UCybBLl24p1L68lW_gK-UdYQ`, read from the channel page by `a3g-youtube-check.mjs`); all 91 ids for which oEmbed answered 200 name the channel CUAHSI as author. The channel's public RSS feed (its 15 latest uploads, `a3g-youtube-feed-261106.csv`) has 11 entries published on 19 October 2026, 1 on 9 October and 3 on 7 October; all 15 are linked from legacy pages, which supports the legacy site's statement that the WARR archive was moved to the channel. The legacy link text equals the video's title for 62 of 75 ids; the other 13 are 11 embedded frames with no link text, one link whose text is "here" and one whose wording differs ("Flow Vegetation Organic Carbon Feedback" against YouTube's "Flow Vegetation-Organic-Carbon").
8. **One matched pair disagrees about the video (P4: reported, not resolved).** The legacy page for the 12 November 2025 session "Post-Field Season Data Practices for Research Success" links video `eqPJlOQcZGc`, whose title on YouTube is "Proactive Data Management for Post-Field Season Data Success" (the title of the May 2025 sessions), while the new item for that session links `unrdDmNL4mQ`, titled "Post-Field Season Data Practices for Research Success". The new item looks right; the legacy page may link the earlier session's video. A person who attended can say.
